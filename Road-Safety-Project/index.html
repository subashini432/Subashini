Key Findings

1. Friday Most Dangerous — 16,444 accidents recorded on Fridays — highest of all days. Sunday safest with 11,388.
2. Rush Hour Peak — Accidents peak at 5PM (8,784) and 4PM (8,487) coinciding with evening rush hour traffic.
3. Urban vs Rural — Urban areas had more accidents (67,304) but Rural roads had higher fatal rate — speed is the key factor.
4. Single Carriageway Risk — Single carriageway roads account for the majority of all accidents despite lower speed limits.
5. Driver Age — Drivers aged 26-35 most frequently involved (35,205). Older drivers 56-65 showed higher fatality rate relative to accident count.
6. Weather Paradox — Most accidents occurred in fine weather — overconfidence in good conditions is a key risk factor.
7. Daily Fatalities — Approximately 4 people die on UK roads every single day based on 2024 data.
